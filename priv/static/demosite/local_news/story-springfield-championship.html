Springfield High Claims Regional Championship

By David Walsh
Published November 22, 2025 at 5:12 PM

The Springfield Eagles secured their third consecutive regional title with a commanding 42-28 victory over arch-rivals Central High on Friday night, capping a season that local fans are already calling one of the program's finest.

Quarterback Jake Morrison threw for 315 yards and four touchdowns in the decisive win, connecting with senior receiver Marcus Hale for three scoring strikes and finding tight end Evan Brooks for the fourth.

"We talked all week about finishing," Morrison said after the game, still catching his breath on the sideline. "Central is tough, they always are, but our offensive line gave me time and the receivers made plays. This one's for the seniors."

A Rivalry Night to Remember

Memorial Stadium was packed well before kickoff, with alumni and students filling both sides of the stands. Central jumped ahead 7-0 on their opening drive, but Springfield answered immediately — Morrison hit Hale for a 48-yard touchdown on the next possession, and the Eagles never trailed again.

Defensively, linebacker Sophia Grant led Springfield with 11 tackles and a forced fumble that set up a short-field score late in the third quarter. Head coach Ray Delgado credited the unit's preparation after Central's early success.

"We settled in after that first series," Delgado said. "The kids trusted the call sheet and played physical. That's championship football."

What's Next

Springfield advances to the state semifinals next weekend against the winner of Saturday's West Region final. Practice resumes Monday at the high school field, with Delgado promising a short celebration and a quick return to film study.

For many in Springfield, Friday night was more than a scoreboard — it was another chapter in a budding dynasty. Three straight regional titles puts the Eagles in rare company in conference history, and the community is already lining up for ticket requests.
